## 📝 Description

EcoQuest is an interactive web-based platform built on the Express.js framework, designed to gamify environmental sustainability. The application encourages users to embark on a digital journey of eco-friendly 'quests,' turning daily green habits into an engaging and rewarding experience. By utilizing a high-performance backend, EcoQuest provides a responsive and seamless environment where users can track their ecological footprint, complete challenges, and learn impactful ways to contribute to a healthier planet. Whether you are looking to reduce waste or optimize energy consumption, EcoQuest transforms sustainable living into an accessible and adventurous quest for all.
